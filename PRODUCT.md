# La MAXIM

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

User-approved: Next.js, React, TypeScript, Vercel, Supabase. Build directly in code; user chose code-first.

## Users

Romanian-speaking visitors entering a giveaway for an event for up to 70 people at La MAXIM in Răstolțu Mare, Sălaj.

## Product Purpose

One focused registration page. Visitors supply surname, first name and a phone number, accept the rules and acknowledge the privacy notice. No SMS or account required.

## Capabilities and Constraints

One entry per normalized phone. Server-side validation, private participant data, persistent abuse limits. Closing time: 2026-12-03T00:00:00+02:00 (exclusive), Europe/Bucharest. Winner selection is external. Cabin use and decor are provisional prize details; organizer must finalize them and legal documents before enabling live entries.

## Brand Commitments

La MAXIM. Ivory, deep green and discreet copper. Serif headings, readable sans body. Elegant, welcoming and restrained. Headline: “Un eveniment al tău. Un loc doar pentru voi.”

## Evidence on Hand

Two user-supplied photographs, copied unchanged from Desktop into public/images: daytime ceremony and evening pond/pavilion. No invented venue facilities, testimonials or participation counts.

## Product Principles

Make entry easy; let the real venue earn trust; keep prize claims precise; protect participant data; never claim a registration succeeded without a database confirmation.
